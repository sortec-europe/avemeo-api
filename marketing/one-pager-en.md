# Avemeo – screens, hotel TV and in-store audio, now also from your AI assistant

**Avemeo** is a cloud platform that manages in-store screens, hotel TV, interactive menus and in-store music and spots from one place. Now you can also run it from the AI assistant you already use.

## Who it is for

- **Retail chains** – music, spots and video campaigns in dozens of stores, with proof of play for every device.
- **Hotels** – TV with live channels, guest name, an interactive services menu, Netflix, YouTube and Chromecast.
- **Companies and institutions** – reception and lobby screens, congress wayfinding, digital menu boards.

## What Avemeo does

| | |
|---|---|
| Content | visual screen editor, templates, playlists, schedules by weekday, time and public holidays |
| Interactivity | multi-level menus for remote control or touch, multiple languages with a language switch |
| Hotel TV | live TV channels with EPG, hotel system integration (guest, room, check-in, check-out) |
| Devices | LG, Samsung, Vestel, Android, Raspberry Pi, ARRIS IPTV set-top boxes |
| Operations | remote power, reboot, volume, screenshots, outage alerts by e-mail and SMS |
| Statistics | per-minute device state, what played and when, CSV export |

## New: Avemeo in your AI assistant

The Avemeo connector links your account to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools. Instead of clicking, just write:

- *"Add the new offer to the reception playlist from Friday, 10 seconds per slide."*
- *"Which players were offline yesterday during opening hours?"*
- *"Create an English version of the hotel menu."*

The assistant works only with your account and the permissions you grant at sign-in. Every change is shown for approval first and can be undone. Deleting through AI is not possible.

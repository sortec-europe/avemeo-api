# Avemeo connects digital signage and hotel TV to AI assistants

**Subheading:** Customers will manage screens, playlists and players in plain language from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini – through the open Model Context Protocol.

**<!-- TODO: city -->, <!-- TODO: date --> –** Avemeo, the cloud platform for digital signage, hotel TV and in-store audio, is launching a connector for AI assistants. Customers can manage their screens, playlists and devices in plain language from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other tools that support the open Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard.

Instead of clicking through the system, users simply tell their assistant, for example, "add the new offer to the reception playlist from Friday" or "which players were offline yesterday". The assistant prepares the change, shows it for approval and saves it once confirmed. Every change is written to the audit log and can be undone.

## About the Model Context Protocol

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard introduced by Anthropic in 2024. It lets AI assistants use external tools and data securely and is supported by Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools. More at [modelcontextprotocol.io](https://modelcontextprotocol.io).

# Avemeo – digital signage, hotel TV and in-store audio, now also from your AI assistant

> 🇸🇰 Slovenská verzia: [one-pager-sk.md](one-pager-sk.md) · 🇨🇿 Česká verze: [one-pager-cs.md](one-pager-cs.md) · Documentation: [github.com/sortec-europe/avemeo-api](https://github.com/sortec-europe/avemeo-api)

**Avemeo** is a cloud platform that manages in-store screens, hotel TV, interactive menus and in-store music and spots from one place. Coming soon: run it from the AI assistant you already use – Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and others.

## Key facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Cloud CMS for digital signage, hotel TV (hospitality IPTV) and in-store audio/video |
| **Where** | Avemeo cloud (`ds.avemeo.com`) and Business Stream by Slovak Telekom (`cloud.tvstream.sk`) |
| **Devices** | LG (webOS), Samsung and Vestel displays · Android · Raspberry Pi · ARRIS IPTV set-top boxes |
| **Integration** | Open REST API (OpenAPI 3, 380+ operations) · AI connector (MCP) for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini – in development |
| **Partner in Slovakia** | SORTEC EUROPE s.r.o. – sales, deployment, support, pilot program · [avemeo@sortec.sk](mailto:avemeo@sortec.sk) |

## Who it is for

- **Retail chains** – music, spots and video campaigns in dozens of stores, with proof of play for every device.
- **Hotels** – TV with live channels, guest name, an interactive services menu, Netflix, YouTube and Chromecast.
- **Companies and institutions** – reception and lobby screens, congress wayfinding, digital menu boards, queue systems.

## What Avemeo does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content** | visual screen editor, templates, playlists, schedules by weekday, time and public holidays |
| **Interactivity** | multi-level menus for remote control or touch, multiple languages with a language switch |
| **Hotel TV** | live TV channels with EPG, hotel system (PMS) integration: guest, room, check-in, check-out |
| **Devices** | LG, Samsung, Vestel, Android, Raspberry Pi, ARRIS IPTV set-top boxes |
| **Operations** | remote power, reboot, volume, screenshots, outage alerts by e-mail and SMS |
| **Statistics** | per-minute device state, what played and when, CSV export |

## Coming soon: Avemeo in your AI assistant

The Avemeo connector links your account to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools through the open Model Context Protocol (MCP). Instead of clicking, just write:

- *"Add the new offer to the reception playlist from Friday, 10 seconds per slide."*
- *"Which players were offline yesterday during opening hours?"*
- *"Create an English version of the hotel menu."*

The assistant works only with your account and the permissions you grant at sign-in. Every change is shown for approval first and can be undone. Deleting through AI is not possible.

For customers without their own AI tool, the Avemeo team is also building an AI assistant directly into the CMS.

The connector (`https://mcp.avemeo.com/mcp`, for Business Stream `https://mcp.tvstream.sk/mcp`) and the CMS assistant are in development; a pilot with selected customers is being prepared. Interested in the pilot: [avemeo@sortec.sk](mailto:avemeo@sortec.sk).

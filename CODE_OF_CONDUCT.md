# Code of conduct

This is a documentation repository maintained by SORTEC EUROPE s.r.o. for customers, partners and developers working with Avemeo. We expect everyone who takes part – in issues, pull requests and discussions – to be respectful and constructive.

- Be welcoming and patient; people come here with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss the content, not the person. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory language are not.
- Do not post confidential information, customer data, credentials or anything you are not allowed to share.
- Respect the maintainers' decisions about what belongs in this repository.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent to [avemeo@sortec.sk](mailto:avemeo@sortec.sk). The maintainers may edit, remove or lock contributions that do not follow these rules.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
